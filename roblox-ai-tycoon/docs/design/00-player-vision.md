## What the owner wants — keep / change

### ✅ Keep
- **The computer is the hub.** You do everything from an in-world computer.
- Save slots / multiple companies on the title screen.
- Daily goals with cash rewards.
- Bank: valuation, set your AI's price, **investors** offering money for equity, **balance sheet** (income vs costs).
- Models tab: all your models, their score, how many users, and an **Update** button.

### 🔧 Change / improve
1. **Bigger office, but few visible employees.** Most staff are not physical NPCs — you **talk to them through the computer** (chat app / messages, like Slack). A few key people can be visible.
2. **Better-looking computer OS.** The retro style is acceptable, but it should look more polished.
3. **Training: many options, not "pick one per category".** Multi-select and mixes, e.g. a data-mix with percentages, several post-training steps, architecture choices, reasoning, safety, tools/agents, multimodal (see research `10-training-options.md`).
4. **Model updates with many options:** smarter (more training), new features (vision, voice, tools, longer context, reasoning mode, agents, cheaper/faster distilled versions, etc.).
5. **A consumer product app**, like a real AI chat app (think "your own chat app"), with plans/tiers, users, features.
6. **Benchmarks vs other models**, with a leaderboard against the real labs.
7. **Difficulty: fairly easy** (not trivially). In **1–2 hours of play you can be among the top AI companies in the world.**
8. **Parts/hardware rework.** The reference has unrealistic prices and it's not fun. Ours: real, year-appropriate hardware and prices (2023 → future), presented in a fun way.
9. **Bank should look good**: charts, valuation history, investor cards, clear P&L.
10. **Fun first.** Everything should feel good to do.

### ⏱️ Time system (from *Build a Phone Empire*)
- Game starts **January 2023**. Hardware, rivals, prices and models are correct for the current in-game date, and the world moves forward month by month, into the future (2027+).
- **Date always visible at the top-right.**
- **Speed controls: ⏸ / x1 / x2 / x4.** Faster time is riskier: things can break or go wrong if you aren't watching (missed events, outages, poached staff, failed training runs).
- **Pacing target:** about 2023 → late 2026 (~46 months) in **~1.5 hours at x1**, which is about **2 minutes per in-game month** (~4 s per in-game day). x4 makes a month take about 30 s. To tune later.
